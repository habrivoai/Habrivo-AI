BEFORE YOU START

A few useful details.

Does signing up include free AI generation?

Eligible new accounts receive exactly 3 lifetime attempts after email verification, not at signup. Attempts never renew. Generation also requires an available, configured model provider and is subject to cooldown and shared daily capacity.

What counts toward my allowance?

A reserved generation attempt counts, including provider failures or timeouts. A cooldown and shared daily service capacity also apply. Saving or copying a pack does not use another attempt.

Can I publish the copy immediately?

You are responsible for reviewing accuracy, product claims, suitability, and platform requirements. AI-generated content can contain mistakes.

Will choosing a plan activate my subscription?

No. Choose a plan, create an account or log in, and continue to checkout when available. Access is not activated by a URL, account creation, or checkout initiation; verified payment confirmation is required.
