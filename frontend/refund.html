Refund & Cancellation Policy

Habrivo AI

Last Updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains how subscription cancellations and refund requests are handled for Habrivo AI services.

1. Subscription Plans

Habrivo AI offers Starter, Pro, and Premium subscription plans with monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, and yearly billing options.

Applicable prices, billing intervals, and renewal information are presented before purchase.

2. Subscription Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of future subscription renewals through the available subscription management process or by contacting customer support.

Cancellation does not automatically result in a refund of payments already completed.

3. Refund Eligibility

Refund requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis. Refunds may be considered in situations such as duplicate payments, incorrect charges, failure to activate a paid subscription, or significant technical issues that prevent access to purchased services.

A change of mind does not automatically qualify for a refund. All requests are subject to review, applicable consumer protection laws, and relevant payment provider requirements.

Nothing in this policy limits any mandatory refund or consumer rights provided by applicable law.

5. Refund Processing

Approved refunds, where applicable, will be processed through the original payment method or another method permitted by the payment provider. Processing times may vary.

6. Contact Support

For refund requests, subscription cancellations, duplicate payments, incorrect charges, or billing issues, please contact Habrivo AI support.

Official Support Email: habrivoai@gmail.com

Please include your registered email address, transaction reference, and a brief description of the issue. Do not share your card number, password, or other sensitive payment details.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this policy as our services and applicable requirements change. Updates will be published here.
